Novaire takes a yield-bearing position (currently: USDC supplied to a Blend Capital lending pool) and splits it into two tradeable tokens:

- **PT (Principal Token)** — a claim on the underlying principal, redeemable in value terms at maturity (subject to a pro-rata cap — see [Settlement](/protocol/settlement)).
- **YT (Yield Token)** — a claim on the yield generated by that principal between now and maturity.

This lets a holder separate "I want my principal back at a fixed date" from "I want exposure to the yield," and lets a market price each side independently through an on-chain AMM.

## What problem this solves

A yield-bearing deposit normally forces a single choice: hold the whole position and take whatever variable yield the underlying market pays. Novaire lets that be decomposed:

- Someone who wants certainty can hold PT and know its approximate redemption value ahead of time.
- Someone who wants leveraged yield exposure without locking up principal can hold YT.
- A market maker can provide liquidity to the PT/SY AMM and earn the swap fee.
