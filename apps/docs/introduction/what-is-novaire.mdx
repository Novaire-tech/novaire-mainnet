## Start with the deposit

You deposit USDC. Novaire doesn't hold it directly — it supplies it into a real [Blend Capital](https://blend.capital) lending pool through a thin wrapper contract (`sy-wrapper`). In exchange you receive **SY** (Standardized Yield) shares, a token that represents your share of that growing pool position.

SY is deliberately generic: it's just "yield-bearing asset, standardized." Everything downstream of it — splitting, trading, settlement — operates on SY, not on Blend directly, so the split/AMM logic doesn't need to know anything about how the yield is produced.

## Split it

The `tokenizer` contract takes SY and mints two tokens against it, in equal face amounts:

- **PT** — redeemable for the underlying value at maturity, regardless of what happens to the yield rate in between (subject to the pro-rata cap described in [Settlement](/protocol/settlement)).
- **YT** — accrues the yield generated by that same SY between now and maturity, or until you sell it.

Minting never fails for lack of collateral — the tokenizer escrows the SY deposited and there's no separate solvency check gate at mint time (a deliberate design choice, not an oversight — see [Trust Boundaries](/security/trust-boundaries)).

## Trade it

The `amm` contract prices PT against SY on a time-decaying curve modeled on Notional Finance's approach: as maturity approaches, the curve gets steeper, converging PT's price toward its redemption value. YT isn't priced by a separate curve — a YT trade is synthesized in the same transaction by flash-splitting or flash-recombining through the tokenizer, so YT pricing is entirely derived from the PT/SY curve plus the live SY rate.

## Settle it

At maturity, the last observed SY rate is frozen. PT redeems against that frozen rate. If the rate regressed unexpectedly right before freeze, the shortfall is shared pro-rata across PT holders rather than paid first-come-first-served — see [Settlement](/protocol/settlement) for the exact mechanics.

## What Novaire is not (today)

- Not multi-market or multi-epoch — this deployment is one market, one maturity, one underlying asset. The current `contracts/` workspace has exactly six crates (`sy-wrapper`, `blend-adapter`, `tokenizer`, `pt-token`, `yt-token`, `amm`); there is no factory, vault, marketplace, rollover, or intent-engine contract.
- Not audited by a third party.
- Not on mainnet.
